## Prototype Definition and Boundaries

The current RI prototype (`RI/prototype/`, `RI/run_prototype.py`, `RI/experiments/E9_heldout_rule_generalization/`, `RI/experiments/E10_guarded_subprimitive_synthesis/`, and `RI/experiments/E11_active_disambiguation/`) is a modular, CPU-first, deterministically testable research prototype implementing bounded hypothesis search, active version-space boundary querying (M13), multi-operator skill composition (M14), guarded continual drift/noise handling (M15), relational object-scene generalization with out-of-grammar abstention (M16), 6-stage guarded sub-primitive synthesis (M17), and active experimental disambiguation with semantic equivalence and unidentifiability detection (M18). It is **not** a general intelligence system, does not execute arbitrary untrusted code, and does not perform open-ended program synthesis.

## Next Research Gates (M19+)

1. **Adaptive Sequential Confidence Bounds for Stopping, Abstention, and Drift (M19):** Replace fixed validation thresholds (`min_val_accuracy = 0.85`) and fixed integer corroboration margins (`min_noisy_corroboration_margin = 2`) with sample-size-aware sequential likelihood-ratio / concentration bounds (Wald SPRT / empirical Bernstein).
2. **Hierarchical Multi-Step Experimental Planning (M20):** Extend one-step myopic information gain to multi-step lookahead experimental trees when intervention costs vary across scene manipulations.
