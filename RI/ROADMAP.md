## Prototype Definition and Boundaries

The current RI prototype (`RI/prototype/` and `RI/run_prototype.py`) is a modular, CPU-first, deterministically testable research prototype implementing a 10-step loop over bounded discrete grid tasks, augmented with active version-space boundary querying (M13), multi-operator skill composition (M14), and guarded continual drift/noise handling (M15). It is **not** a general intelligence system, does not execute arbitrary untrusted code, and does not perform open-ended program synthesis.

## Next Research Gates (M16+)

1. **Joint Active Querying under Label Noise (M16):** Combine M13 (`ActiveBoundaryQuerier`) and M15 (`DriftAndNoiseController`) using repeated/confirming queries when oracle responses are noisy (`noise_prob > 0`).
2. **Hierarchical Depth-3+ Relational DAGs (M17):** Extend M14 beyond depth-2 binary Boolean operators to multi-step DAGs with intermediate latent predicates and automatic sub-skill discovery.
3. **Distribution-Free Drift Confidence Bounds (M18):** Replace fixed empirical thresholds (`noise_tolerance = 0.20`, `max_adapt_batch_error = 0.22`) with adaptive sequential hypothesis tests (such as Page-Hinkley or Hoeffding bounds) that scale automatically with batch size.
