# RESEARCH PLAN

## Phase 1 (current): Foundations and E1

1. Define intelligence questions operationally.
2. Literature comparison (causal learning, predictive processing, active learning, developmental psychology).
3. Implement and execute E1: relationship formation + transfer in simulated world.
4. Evaluate against baselines; classify H1 as supported / refuted / unclear.

## Phase 2 (proposed after E1 evidence)

- Refine environment so that interaction rules cannot be solved by base rates.
- Implement explicit hypothesis revision / competition.
- Measure sample efficiency and robustness to noise.
- Add simple multi-step consequence examination.

## Evaluation Principles

- Pre-register metrics and splits before interpreting.
- Separate train and held-out combinations.
- Report runtime, seeds, failures.
- No claim of intelligence until operational criteria met across multiple experiments.
