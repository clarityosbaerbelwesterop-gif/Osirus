# Related Approaches

| Approach | Core mechanism | Relevance to RI | Known limits |
|----------|----------------|-----------------|--------------|
| Classic rule learning / ILP | Explicit symbolic rules | Close to relationship formation | Brittle, needs language bias |
| Bayesian causal models | Posterior over graphs | Strong comparison for H1 | Computational cost grows |
| Neural world models | Latent dynamics prediction | Prediction + update | Black-box, presupposes architecture |
| Developmental robotics | Embodied exploration | Stage 1 analogy | Hardware dependent |
| Large language models | Next-token prediction on massive data | Explicitly *not* the starting point | Pre-trained, not grown from simple state |

RI seeks a mechanism whose power emerges from relationship revision rather than from scale of pre-training or fixed architecture.
