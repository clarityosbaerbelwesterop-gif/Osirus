# Safety and Boundaries

- No unrestricted shell, network, or secret access granted to any experimental agent.
- No self-modification of safety controls, evaluation protocols, or isolation rules.
- Recursive improvement proposals must include versioned comparison, regression tests, and explicit rollback.
- No paid resources without human approval.
- All RI work remains under `RI/` on branch `ri/isolated-foundation`.
- Claims of intelligence require operational evidence meeting pre-defined criteria; none are made.
