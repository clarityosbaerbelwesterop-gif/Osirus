# SKILL: Open Design — Frontier AI Lab UI & UX Polish

## Aesthetic & UX Standards (Anthropic / OpenAI / Apple Level)
1. **Editorial Clarity & Calm Authority**:
   - Crisp typography, generous whitespace, subtle hairline borders (`1px solid`), and zero decorative noise or "coming soon" badges.
   - No intrusive cookie banners or draft disclaimers on legal/public pages when strictly-necessary storage is used.
   - No cluttered FAQ accordions on the primary landing page; the product architecture and live capabilities speak for themselves.
2. **Responsive & Mobile-First Precision**:
   - Touch targets >= 44x44px, zero horizontal overflow at 320px-430px viewports, stable layout without CLS during streaming or model switching.
3. **Tactile Feedback & Honest States**:
   - Disabled states during in-flight submissions (prevent double-submit chaos), clear quota progress bars (5h & Weekly windows), and instant keyboard navigation (`Cmd/Ctrl+K`, `Enter` to send, `Escape` to close).
